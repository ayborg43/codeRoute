Provider Keys

Paste a key to enable a provider. Keys are stored encrypted in Postgres and checked against the provider before they are saved, so a typo is caught here rather than by every completion afterwards. Saving a key also reads that provider’s model list. free tier marks providers that offer usable free access. A few publish their model list without authentication — there a key is stored but cannot be proven, and shows as unverified.
